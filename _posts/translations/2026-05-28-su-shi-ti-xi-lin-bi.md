横看成岭侧成峰 <br />
远近高低各不同 <br />
不识庐山真面目 <br />
只缘身在此山中

Afront one sees a ridge aside it peaks <br />
At odds before behind above below <br />
One cannot see the truth of Mount Lu’s cheeks <br />
Because one is between one cannot know
