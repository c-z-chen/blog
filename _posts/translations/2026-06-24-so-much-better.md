**《越来越好》** <br />
房子大了电话小了 感觉越来越好 <br />
假期多了工资高了 工作越来越好 <br />
商品精了价格活了 心情越来越好 <br />
天更蓝了水更清呀 环境越来越好 <br />

**So Much Better** <br />
Houses bigger, phones are smaller; life is so much better <br />
Breaks are longer, wages higher; work is so much better <br />
Things are finer, prices fairer; feeling so much better <br />
The sky is bluer, lakes are clearer; my world is so much better <br />
