**《无题》（相见时难别亦难）** <br />
相见时难别亦难 [xiāng jiàn shí nán bié yì nán]<br />
东风无力百花残 [dōng fēng wú lì bǎi huā cán]<br />
春蚕到死丝方尽 [chūn cán dào sǐ sī fāng jìn]<br />
蜡炬成灰泪始干 [là jù chéng huī lèi shǐ gān]<br />
晓镜但愁云鬓改 [xiǎo jìng dàn chóu yún bìn gǎi]<br />
夜吟应觉月光寒 [yè yín yīng jué yuè guāng hán]<br />
蓬山此去无多路 [péng shān cǐ qù wú duō lù]<br />
青鸟殷勤为探看 [qīng niǎo yīn qín wèi tàn kàn]<br />

**Untitled (xiāng jiàn shí nán)** <br />
To tryst is hard, and hard too is to part<br />
East zephyrs waning blossoms fall apart<br />
The silk worms spin their silk till they must die<br />
The wax to ash will burn and tears will dry<br />
Dawn glass alas has dark locks turned to grey<br />
Night chants should feel the chill of moon's cold ray<br />
Mount Peng to reach is not so far a way<br />
Keen blue bird search and scout the path I pray<br />
