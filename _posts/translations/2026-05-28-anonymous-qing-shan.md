我问青山何日老，青山问我何时闲。 

I ask the verd’rous mount when ’twill be mellow
The verd’rous mount asks when I’ll mellow out

# ---
# 
# Latin *viridis* ‘green’ -> **Verdurous**: lush green vegetation; *literary* the fresh green colour of lush vegetation; *literary* a condition of freshness. Keats, ‘Ode to a Nightingale’: ‘Through verdurous glooms and winding mossy ways’ or Milton, ‘The verdurous wall of paradise up sprung’. &, indeed, **Mellow**: also Keats, ‘Season of mists and mellow fruitfulness’; Donne, ‘To ripe and mellow here, we’re stubborn clay’; Lord Tennyson, ‘But thro’ the slowly-mellowing avenues / And solitary passes of the wood’; Wordsworth ‘A freshening lustre mellow’. Lastly, **Mellow out**: *informal* relax and enjoy oneself.
