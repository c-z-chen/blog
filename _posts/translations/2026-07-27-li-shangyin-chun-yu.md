春雨。<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈按此诗亦无题之类。〉</span><br />
怅卧新春白袷衣，<span style="color: #F54329">〈怅，Glum。卧，lit. Recline, 'lain in bed'。新春，New spring。白袷衣，lit. White double-sided jacket, a garb of thin white lined fabric, which was a light and informal garment often worn by people at home during the Tang Dynasty, 'white tunic gown'.〉</span>白门寥落意多违。<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈说文：怅，望，恨也。李贺染丝上春机诗：白祫玉郎寄桃叶，为君挑鸾作腰绶。白袷，春衣也。寥落寂寞意多违，不遂其愿也。〉</span><span style="color: #EACD1E">〈﹝桉﹞淮南子：八极之西，南方曰编驹，之山曰白门，必非所用。魏志吕布传：彭城有白门楼。南史：建康宣阳门谓之白门。水经注：邺城有七门，西曰白门，亦非所用。此似取白门楬柳之。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈白门，White Gate。寥落，lit. Forlorn, 'bereft' as a double entendre (a hypallage) that foreshadows later introduction of a faraway person。意，lit. Intents/desire/wishes, 'those pined for hopes'。多违，lit. Are mostly violated, 'are down'.〉</span>红楼隔雨<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈于鹄诗何处少年吹玉笛，谁家鹦鹉语红楼。〉</span>相望冷，<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈两相遥望，为风雨所隔，故觉冷然。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈红楼，lit. Red mansion/tower/building, 'Red Hall'。隔雨，lit. With the rain in between, 'beyond the rain'。相望 lit. Look at each other, 'looks back'。冷，Cold, also aloof。〉</span>珠箔飘灯<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈汉武故事：武帝起神室，以白珠织为箔，玳瑁压之。李白诗：美人一笑牵珠箔，遥指红楼是妾家。〉</span>独自归。<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈不能同往也。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈珠箔，lit. Thin layers (films) of pearl, said to be woven and weighed down with tortoiseshell in Emperor Wu's sacred chamber, possibly a allusion to Li Bai's poem 'To a Belle on the Road' which compares a beautiful lady's smile to *pearl screens*, whose residence is a *Red Mansion*, 'pearl screens'。飘灯，lit. Fluttering/floating/hovering lamps, 'stray lamps', a transferrable epithet to the poet's personal straying。独自，lit. Alone by one self, 'one lone'。归，lit. Goes back, 'wends back around'。〉</span>远路应悲春晼晚，<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈腕音宛。楚辞哀时命：白日腕腕其将入兮。室迩人远，故曰远路。〉</span><span style="color: #EACD1E">〈其人远去。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈远路，lit. Far paths, 'far'way', a phonetic shortening of faraway, in consideration of its figurative suggestion of distance in both space or time。应，Shall。悲，Grieve。春，'spring's'。晼晚，lit. Sunset night, a possible allusion to *Chu Ci*, which laments the fate of the times (「白日*腕腕*其将入兮」, lit. 'The white sun is about to set.'), ‘late declining light', 'late' placed here in order retain the possibilty of both late spring and late day。〉</span>残宵犹得梦依稀。<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈人虽不见，而梦犹得依稀聚会也。下文又转路远。〉</span><span style="color: #EACD1E">〈惟梦中可寻。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈残宵，lit. Remaining night, ‘spent night'。犹，lit. As if/yet/even/moreover/still, 'yet'。得，lit. Obtain/can, 'yields'。梦，Dream, 'a dream'。依稀，lit. Vague/dim/faint, 'faint to the mind'。〉</span>玉珰缄札何由达，<span style="color: #93E3FD">〈风俗通：耳珠曰珰。张正见诗：谁论白玉珰。玉珰缄札，犹今所云侑缄。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈玉珰，Jade earrings。缄札，lit. Sealed epistle, 'sealed post'。何由，lit. By what means, 'how might it'。达，lit. Arrive, 'alight', as in 'descending from the air and settle', namely referring to the later avifauna。〉</span>万里云罗一雁飞。<span style="color: #AAAAAA">〈风俗通：耳珠曰：珰，玉珰。缄札，犹今所云侑缄。缄封也。礼书，札也。达，寄也。雁传书鸟也。〉</span><span style="color: #EACD1E">〈末联记私札飞传情之事。〉</span><span style="color: #F54329">〈万里，lit. Ten thousand mile(s), 'ten thousand mile', usually a figure of speech used to mean a long distance, here taken as one whole。云罗，lit. Dense clouds like net, seen earliest in Jiang Yan's *Imitating Ji Kang*'s 'On the Will', where it signifies the all-encompassing net of cosmic order (「旷哉宇宙惠，*云罗*更四陈」or roughly, 'Vast is the universe's bounty, *cloudy nets* spread all around.'), 'clouds hang' (Cf. Poe 'the clouds hung oppressively low in the heavens'), a double entendre where 'hang' may either be read as noun or verb, in the latter case revealing the cloud's oppressive nature。一雁飞，lit. One goose flies, 'one goose in flight'。〉</span>

**《春雨》李商隐** <br />
怅卧新春白袷衣 [chàng wò xīn chūn bái jiā yī] <br />
白门寥落意多违 [bái mén liáo luò yì duō wéi] <br />
红楼隔雨相望冷 [hóng lóu gé yǔ xiāng wàng lěng] <br />
珠箔飘灯独自归 [zhū bó piāo dēng dú zì guī] <br />
远路应悲春晼晚 [yuǎn lù yīng bēi chūn wǎn wǎn] <br />
残宵犹得梦依稀 [cán xiāo yóu dé mèng yī xī] <br />
玉珰缄札何由达 [yù dāng jiān zhā hé yóu dá] <br />
万里云罗一雁飞 [wàn lǐ yún luó yí yàn fēi] <br />

**Spring Rain, by Li Shangyin** <br />
Glum lain in bed new spring white tunic-gown<br />
White Gate bereft those pined for hopes are down<br />
Red Hall beyond the rain looks back in cold<br />
Pearl screens stray lamps one lone wends back around<br />
Far'way shall grieve spring's late declining light<br />
Spent night yet yields a dream faint to the mind<br />
Jade ear-ring sealed post how might it alight<br />
Ten thousand mile clouds hang one goose in flight
