**《义勇军进行曲》** <br />
起来！不愿做奴隶的人们！<br />
把我们的血肉筑成我们新的长城！<br />
中华民族到了最危险的时候，<br />
每个人被迫着发出最后的吼声。<br />
起来！起来！起来！<br />
我们万众一心，<br />
冒着敌人的炮火，前进！<br />
冒着敌人的炮火，前进！<br />
前进！前进、进！

**March of the Volunteers; or, March of The Righteous and Brave Fighters** <br />
Arise! The People who’d scorn to be slaves! <br />
Take up our flesh, blood; wherewith our new Great Wall we’ll raise! <br />
The Chinese Nation is at—its most perilous moment; <br />
We all have to let out roars—they’ll be our very last. <br />
Arise! Arise! Arise! <br />
All our hearts in one mind <br />
Brave the enemy’s gunfire, March on! <br />
Brave the enemy’s gunfire, March on! <br />
March on! March on! March on! On!
